VeggieTales!

There's never ever, ever, ever, ever been a show like VeggieTales!


VeggieTales is an animated TV series
that was originally intended to convey Christian moral themes
and teach Biblical values and lessons for a child-based audience.


The first episode was, "Where's God When I'm S-Scared?"
and it was first released onto Video Home System (VHS) videocassette on 23 December 1993.

About 2 or 3 episodes were originally released per year, from 1993 to 2015.
Other various shows continued to be released until 2022.


The success of the videos led to multiple spin-off television series,
such as, VeggieTales on TV!,
which ran on the National Broadcasting Company (NBC) TV network
on Saturday mornings from 2006 to 2009,
and then there were two Netflix series that debuted in 2014 and 2017,
and a series produced by Trinity Broadcasting Network (TBN) in 2019.
Two films were released:
Jonah: A VeggieTales Movie (2002)
and The Pirates Who Don't Do Anything: A VeggieTales Movie (2008).
The success of the animations helped establish a franchise of related media,
including books, music, live stage productions, and video games.

According to Wikipedia, the free encyclopaedia,
as of September of 2026,
the series has sold over 16 million books, 7 million music CDs,
and 235 million music streams.

The last known releases continued to happen monthly from 2019 to 2022.
The VeggieTales Show had new episodes monthly from 2019 to 2022 and they
were shown on the Yippee TV subscription streaming service.

Mark Altrogge is an American Christian songwriter and pastor from Indiana, Pennsylvania,
whose music career grew out of leading worship at his church after getting saved in college.
He began writing worship songs in the 1970s and has published more than 150 songs,
including "I Stand in Awe," "One Pure and Holy Passion," "In the Presence,"
and "I'm Forever Grateful."

In 1985, he wrote the song, "I'm Forever Grateful."
It became popular when it was recorded by the Christian vocal group, called, Glad,
on their 1989 album, Romans.
It was that 1989 recording that helped introduce it to wider Christian audiences.
Altrogge's other songs have also appeared on albums by Sovereign Grace Ministries,
Integrity Music, and other artists.
He even recorded with his son, Stephen, on a 2007 album titled,
In a Little While, by Mark & Stephen Altrogge.
As of October of 2026, he continues to be primarily a pastor, teacher, and church-focused songwriter.



Glad is a band that started in 1972
and was one of the first bands that
released Christian pop and Christian rock music.
Their fame and popularity increased greatly when they
released their 1988 album, The Acapella Project.
Their a cappella music was so popular in 1988,
that they released more a cappella albums up until 2005.

Starting with their first album in 1978, they had released 23 albums,
plus 6 Compilation albums, and also provided
Instruments and/or background vocals on 10 other albums
by other various artists.

Their last known album was released in 2005.

The 1989 album, Romans, was their ninth album.
It was on their 1989 album, Romans, that we find Glad's famous recording for, I'm Forever Grateful.
