VeggieTales!

There's never ever, ever, ever, ever been a show like VeggieTales!


VeggieTales is an animated TV series
that was originally intended to convey Christian moral themes
and teach Biblical values and lessons for a child-based audience.


The first episode was, "Where's God When I'm S-Scared?"
and it was first released onto Video Home System (VHS) videocassette on 23 December 1993.

About 2 or 3 episodes were originally released per year, from 1993 to 2015.
Other various shows continued to be released until 2022.


The success of the videos led to multiple spin-off television series,
such as, VeggieTales on TV!,
which ran on the National Broadcasting Company (NBC) TV network
on Saturday mornings from 2006 to 2009,
and then there were two Netflix series that debuted in 2014 and 2017,
and a series produced by Trinity Broadcasting Network (TBN) in 2019.
Two films were released:
Jonah: A VeggieTales Movie (2002)
and The Pirates Who Don't Do Anything: A VeggieTales Movie (2008).
The success of the animations helped establish a franchise of related media,
including books, music, live stage productions, and video games.

According to Wikipedia, the free encyclopaedia,
as of September of 2026,
the series has sold over 16 million books, 7 million music CDs,
and 235 million music streams.

The last known releases continued to happen monthly from 2019 to 2022.
The VeggieTales Show had new episodes monthly from 2019 to 2022 and they
were shown on the Yippee TV subscription streaming service.

Mark Altrogge is an American Christian songwriter and pastor from Indiana, Pennsylvania,
whose music career grew out of leading worship at his church after getting saved in college.
He began writing worship songs in the 1970s and has published more than 150 songs,
including "I Stand in Awe," "One Pure and Holy Passion," "In the Presence,"
and "I'm Forever Grateful."

In 1985, he wrote the song, "I'm Forever Grateful."
It became popular when it was recorded by the Christian vocal group, called, Glad,
on their 1989 album, Romans.
It was that 1989 recording that helped introduce it to wider Christian audiences.
Altrogge's other songs have also appeared on albums by Sovereign Grace Ministries,
Integrity Music, and other artists.
He even recorded with his son, Stephen, on a 2007 album titled,
In a Little While, by Mark & Stephen Altrogge.
As of October of 2026, he continues to be primarily a pastor, teacher, and church-focused songwriter.

and was one of the first bands that
released Christian pop and Christian rock music.
Their fame and popularity increased greatly when they
released their 1988 album, The Acapella Project.
Their a cappella music was so popular in 1988,
that they released more a cappella albums up until 2005.

Starting with their first album in 1978, they had released 23 albums,
plus 6 Compilation albums, and also provided
Instruments and/or background vocals on 10 other albums

The 1989 album, Romans, was their ninth album.
It was on their 1989 album, Romans, that we find Glad's famous recording for, I'm Forever Grateful.

Here is a very interesting Christian song that has very interesting words.
My favourite version is the version found on two 2012 albums,
Paul van Dyk's sixth studio album, Evolution, and another album featuring
Various Artists, titled, Vonyc Sessions 2012. Both of these albums came out in 2012.

This song, "I Don't Deserve You," was created by Paul van Dyk and Plumb.
It had many different versions and remixes in 2012 and 2013.


Matthias Paul, known professionally as Paul van Dyk,
is a German DJ, record producer and musician.
As of October of 2026, he had released over 17 albums,
and over 51 singles.
He also provided music for one film and four video games.
He is also the host of the radio show "VONYC Sessions",
which had reached over 1000 episodes by January of 2026.


Paul van Dyk created a song called, "I Don't Deserve You."
The melody and words of "I Don't Deserve You" were jointly written
by Paul van Dyk and Plumb.
Paul van Dyk is the lead electronic artist/producer, and Plumb is the featured vocalist.
Plumb described the song as inspired by her experience as a mother,
feeling unworthy of the love her children gave her, and by the parallel she saw with God's unconditional love.
God's grace is getting His love that we don't deserve.
Grace is unmerited, undeserved, unearned favour.
Mercy is not getting what we do deserve. (not dying, not going to hell)
Grace is getting what we don't deserve. (receiving His love, being saved and going to heaven)
Going to heaven is done by His grace and not by our works.

Tiffany Arbuckle Lee, better known by her stage name Plumb,
is an American singer-songwriter and author.
She originally started as a contemporary Christian music recording artist in 1997.
Plumb is now known as well as or better in alternative rock
and Electronic Dance Music (EDM), and for the use of her music
in film and television.
As of October of 2026,
She had released nine studio albums, from 1997 through 2024.

Paul van Dyk's album version was the original version of the song.
The song was then remixed into a radio edit and an album version for Plumb's album.

The original 2012 single release is credited to Paul van Dyk featuring Plumb.
The Discogs website lists digital release dates of 8 October 2012 (WAV maxi-single) and 15 October 2012 (MP3 single).

Paul van Dyk released "I Don't Deserve You" featuring Plumb on his sixth studio album,
Evolution, and that album was released on 3 April 2012.

Paul van Dyk later released the song as a single on 25 September 2012.
The 2012 remix package is titled, "I Don't Deserve You (Remixes)," -- and it contains
7 different remixed versions of the song.

"I Don't Deserve You" appears on another album, titled, Vonyc Sessions 2012 (Presented by Paul van Dyk) as the opening track on CD 1,
in the version titled "I Don't Deserve You (Seven Lions, Maor Levi & Bluestone, Original Mashup)".
It's credited to Paul van Dyk feat. Plumb, with Seven Lions, Maor Levi, and Bluestone.

That album is a mixed compilation of various artists, not a Paul van Dyk studio album nor a separate release by Plumb.
In this case, the "Original Mashup" included on the Vonyc Sessions 2012 album is the same as the Paul van Dyk Album Version.
It combines the contributions of Seven Lions, Maor Levi, and Bluestone.
That various artists compilation album is listed as a 2012 release.


To say that in the correct order, the first and original version was the
Paul van Dyk Album Version

That is the original version.
It is also identified as the Original Mashup on the 2012 album,

The Original Mashup is the same as the Paul van Dyk Album Version.
All of the other tracks are remixes. We start to see the remixes get released
about five months after the album, as they first start to get released in September of 2012.

The track gets remixed two more times for Plumb's album in 2013.
The remixes made for her are known as the Plumb Album Version and the Plumb Radio Mix.



Plumb's Album Mix of "I Don't Deserve You" (with Paul van Dyk)

Tom McLain is an American Christian songwriter associated with praise and worship music.

J. Daniel Smith is an American Christian music producer, arranger, orchestrator, keyboardist, and conductor.
A Dove Award-winning musician, he worked with artists including Matthew Ward, Dennis Jernigan,
Geron Davis, and Mark Condon.
Smith led the music and worship ministry at Bethesda Community Church in Fort Worth, Texas,
for 33 years before becoming the church's senior pastor from 2011 until 2024.
